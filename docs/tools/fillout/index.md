# :material-file-document-outline: Fillout

ERRSA's form platform for collecting structured submissions from members, players, and staff.

---

## ERRSA Use

Fillout contains forms used by **ERRSA as a club** as well as forms specific to **ERRSA Minecraft**.

### ERRSA

Broader ERRSA forms may be used for things such as:

- member or committee interest
- concerns and suggestions
- organization feedback
- event or club submissions

### ERRSA Minecraft

Minecraft forms may be used for things such as:

- player reports
- server feedback
- ban appeals
- staff or team interest
- token requests and approvals
- other structured Minecraft submissions

Not every form is part of the Minecraft program, so staff should pay attention to which ERRSA area owns a form before editing or acting on it.

## Core Areas

| Area | Used For |
| --- | --- |
| **Forms** | Viewing and maintaining ERRSA and ERRSA Minecraft forms. |
| **Responses** | Reviewing submitted information. |
| **Logic & Settings** | Controlling form behavior, validation, and submission settings. |
| **Sharing** | Managing the links or access used to distribute a form. |
| **Discord Integration** | Sending supported form submissions or notifications directly to Discord through Fillout's built-in Discord integration. |

---

## Notes

!!! warning "Live forms"
    Changes to a published form can immediately affect members or players using it. Confirm that a change is intended before editing questions, required fields, or submission behavior.

Some forms may feed other ERRSA systems, so avoid changing field names or expected values without checking whether another service depends on them.
