# 3. Standard procedures

Use these procedures to respond consistently to player reports and server incidents. Choose the situation below, take the least severe effective action, and record what happened.


## 3.1 Moderation ability overview

Staff are given moderation abilities appropriate to their role ([Section 2.3](staff-roles-responsibilities.md#23-staff-roles){ data-preview }). These abilities are tools to maintain a fair and friendly server, not privileges for personal use or gain.

General guidelines for using moderation powers:

- If a situation is covered below, follow its specific procedure.

- If not, always use the least severe action necessary (warn → mute → kick → ban).

- Always provide a reason when taking action against a player.

- Document the incident in the approved staff record location and use the Discord staff action process ([Section 4.2](logging-documentation.md#42-send-a-written-warning){ data-preview } or [Section 4.3](logging-documentation.md#43-send-a-disciplinary-action-notice){ data-preview }) for formal notices.

- When in doubt, escalate the issue up the chain of command (see [Section 2.1](staff-roles-responsibilities.md#21-chain-of-command){ data-preview }).


## 3.2 Situation specifics


### Server Crashes

- Immediately notify a Developer or Committee Lead.

- Check Discord staff channels for updates from the technical team.

- Do not attempt fixes beyond your permissions (e.g., restarting the server via Apex if you do not have access).

- If the server remains down for more than 15 minutes, a Developer or Lead should make a public announcement.


### Staff Member Abuses Power

- Collect evidence (screenshots, chat logs, recordings).

- Immediately escalate to the Committee Lead.

- If the Committee Lead is involved, escalate to the Executive Board.

- Do not confront the staff member publicly; handle it internally.


### Inappropriate Language (Chat/Signs/Books)

- In most cases, the auto filter plugin should censor inappropriate language

- If bypassed, issue a verbal warning first if the behavior is minor.

- Remove the offensive signs/books if possible.

- If it continues, issue a temporary mute.

- First offense, 24 hour

- Second offense, 1 week

- Third offense, permanent

- Severe cases (hate speech, slurs, etc.)

- Mods → Issue a permanent mute and escalate to Admin/Lead.

- Admins → If needed, create a formal student conduct report.


### Inappropriate Behavior (Harassment, Toxicity, etc.)

- Issue a verbal warning first if the behavior is minor.

- If offense is through chat, refer to [inappropriate language guidance](#inappropriate-language-chatsignsbooks "Review the chat, sign, and book guidance below")

- If it continues, issue a temporary ban.

- First offense, 24 hour

- Second offense, 1 week

- Third offense, permanent

- In severe cases, escalate to Admin for formal student conduct report

- Document the incident thoroughly in the approved staff record location; use the Discord staff action process ([Section 4.2](logging-documentation.md#42-send-a-written-warning){ data-preview } or [Section 4.3](logging-documentation.md#43-send-a-disciplinary-action-notice){ data-preview }) for a formal notice when required.


### Inappropriate builds

- It may be necessary for an Admin to use investigation tools to determine the player who built the structure.

- Destroy the structure if possible and replace it with a sign stating “Inappropriate builds are a violation of our rules. Please remain respectful on the server.”.

- If the player is offline, and the behavior is minor, the signage described above is sufficient.

- If the player is online, issue a verbal warning if the behavior is minor.

- If it continues, or the issue is more than minor, issue a temporary ban.

- First offense, 24 hour

- Second offense, 1 week

- Third offense, permanent

- In severe cases, escalate to Admin for formal student conduct report

- Document the incident thoroughly in the approved staff record location; use the Discord staff action process ([Section 4.2](logging-documentation.md#42-send-a-written-warning){ data-preview } or [Section 4.3](logging-documentation.md#43-send-a-disciplinary-action-notice){ data-preview }) for a formal notice when required.


### Cheating, Hacking, or Exploiting

- Collect evidence whenever possible (screenshots, videos, plugin alerts).

- Confirm with anti-cheat logs or tools (if available).

- Issue a temporary ban:

- First offense, 1 week

- Second offense, 1 month

- Third offense, permanent

- If the case is extreme, issue a permanent ban immediately.

- Always report exploits that affect server stability or fairness to a Developer.


### Griefing or Theft

- Admins can use investigation tools to determine who caused the damage or took the stolen items and verify claim/ownership of builds or items.

- Some guess work may be necessary, use best discretion and ask, “what is most likely to have occurred?” when both parties can’t agree on the story.

- Admins can restore damages or give items if possible and verifiable

- Issue a verbal warning first if the behavior is minor.

- If it continues, or the issue is more than minor, issue a temporary ban.

- First offense, 24 hour

- Second offense, 1 week

- Third offense, permanent

- Always log rollbacks and punishment actions.


### Spamming or Advertising

- Refer to [inappropriate language guidance](#inappropriate-language-chatsignsbooks "Review the chat, sign, and book guidance below") for further instructions


### Lag Machines

- If accidental (Ex. Making a complex redstone farm), warn the player and ask them to remove the machine.

- If it continues, or the machine was created for intentional lag creation, issue a temporary ban.

- First offense, 24 hours

- Second offense, 1 week

- Third offense, permanent

- Admins may use creative or other plugin tools (such as world edit) to dismantle the machine if needed.

- In severe cases, a server roll back may be needed, but only as a last resort.


### Player Disputes / Drama

- Encourage players to resolve respectfully.

- Step in if rules are being broken (toxic chat, harassment).

- If unresolved, refer to [inappropriate language guidance](#inappropriate-language-chatsignsbooks "Review the chat, sign, and book guidance below") or [inappropriate behavior guidance](#inappropriate-behavior-harassment-toxicity-etc "Review the harassment and toxicity guidance below") based on what is more appropriate.


## 3.3 Ban appeals

- Appeals must be submitted through the official Appeal Form (/report in-game)

- Appeals will be reviewed by the committee lead, and if appropriate for an appeal, voted on by the staff team to determine a decision.

- Staff who issued the punishment may provide additional context if called upon.


## 3.4 Suggestion forms

- Suggestions should be submitted through the official Suggestion Form (/suggest in-game)

- Developers review technical suggestions (plugins, builds, gameplay).

- Committee Lead reviews staff and rule-related suggestions.

- All suggestions should receive a staff response, even if denied, to maintain transparency.
