# 6. Staff Advancement & Governance

This section outlines how staff promotions, demotions, inactivity, Server Lead selection, and organizational oversight are handled within ERRSA MC.

---

## 6.1 Promotions

Promotions are determined at the discretion of the Server Lead. There is no automatic promotion based on activity, time served, or completion of a specific set of requirements.

When considering a promotion, the Server Lead may consider a staff member's:

- Community engagement
- Helpfulness toward players and teammates
- Attention to server policies, philosophy, and rules
- Team collaboration
- Ability to follow through on commitments
- Judgment and communication
- Contributions to events, documentation, or server systems
- Willingness to volunteer for projects
- Willingness to ask for and respond to feedback
- Ability to learn from more experienced staff
- Consistent demonstration of readiness
- Attendance and participation in staff meetings

These factors are used to determine whether a staff member is prepared to take on the responsibilities of a higher role.

!!! important "Promotions are earned through readiness"
    Promotions are earned through showing readiness and team dynamics, not guaranteed by activity alone.

---

## 6.2 Demotions & Removal

Staff members are expected to consistently meet the expectations of their assigned role.

When a staff member is not meeting those expectations, the Server Lead may provide additional guidance or tutoring to help address areas where the staff member is falling short.

Depending on the circumstances, a staff member may be:

- Provided additional guidance or training
- Demoted to a role that better matches their current responsibilities
- Removed from the staff team

Demotion or removal may be considered when a staff member consistently fails to meet the expectations of their role, abuses staff authority, ignores server rules or policies, causes significant disruption within the staff team, or acts contrary to the principles of the server.

Staff removal is generally reserved for serious circumstances and is not intended as a response to ordinary mistakes or temporary difficulties.

---

## 6.3 Staff Inactivity

Staff members may have periods where school, work, or other responsibilities limit their availability. Temporary periods of reduced activity do not automatically result in a change to staff status.

Staff members who expect to be unavailable for an extended period should communicate this to the Server Lead.

Consistent inactivity over an extended period, combined with little or no communication or engagement, may result in demotion. This is intended for situations where it reasonably appears that a staff member has stopped participating in the server rather than a short-term period of reduced activity.

!!! important "Extended absences"
    Staff members who need to step away from ERRSA MC for an extended period should communicate with the Server Lead. An extended absence that has been specifically communicated and agreed upon does not automatically result in demotion.

---

## 6.4 Server Lead Selection

The Server Lead is responsible for leading the Minecraft staff team and has final authority over normal staff and server decisions.

The Server Lead is selected through an annual voting process held toward the end of the spring semester.

The voting process is facilitated by the **ERRSA Executive Board** to keep the selection process separate from the Minecraft Committee and provide an impartial process.

### 6.4.1 Candidate Eligibility

Only current staff members holding one of the following positions may be considered as candidates for Server Lead:

- Server Lead
- Developer
- Admin

Moderators are not eligible to be considered for Server Lead.

### 6.4.2 Voting

Each eligible voter may select their top three candidates.

Votes are weighted according to the voter's preference:

| Choice        | Points |
|---------------|--------|
| First choice  | 3      |
| Second choice | 2      |
| Third choice  | 1      |

The candidate receiving the highest total number of points is selected as Server Lead.

### 6.4.3 Vacancy

If there is no current Server Lead, the entire Developer team will act collectively as Server Lead until a new Server Lead is appointed through the proper voting process.

---

## 6.5 ERRSA Executive Authority

The ERRSA MC Committee operates under the organizational oversight of the ERRSA Executive Board.

The Server Lead is responsible for normal Minecraft server operations, staff management, and server decisions. The ERRSA Executive Board retains authority over the organization's staff positions and may intervene when necessary.

At any point, the **ERRSA Executive Board has full authority to remove any staff member, including the Server Lead, from their position.**

This authority exists independently of the normal promotion, demotion, and Server Lead selection processes.

---

## 6.6 Reporting Staff Concerns

Staff members who believe another staff member is acting outside their authority, violating server expectations, abusing their position, or otherwise acting inappropriately should first communicate the concern to the Server Lead.

If the Server Lead does not adequately address the concern, or if the Server Lead is directly involved in the matter, the concern should be brought to the **ERRSA Executive Board or Advisor**.

!!! important "Staff concerns"
    Concerns involving staff members should be raised through the appropriate leadership structure rather than being handled through public staff or player discussions.

Retaliation against a staff member or student for raising a legitimate concern is not permitted. Depending on the circumstances, retaliatory behavior may result in additional staff action or referral to the appropriate formal student conduct process.
