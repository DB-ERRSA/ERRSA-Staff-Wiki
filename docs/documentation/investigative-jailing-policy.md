# Investigative Jailing Policy

Jailing temporarily restricts a player while staff investigate an incident or prevent further disruption. **Jail is not proof that a player broke a rule, and it is not an extra punishment.** Use this policy alongside [Standard Procedures](standard-procedures.md#32-situation-specifics){ data-preview }, particularly the procedures for [cheating and exploiting](standard-procedures.md#326-cheating-hacking-or-exploiting){ data-preview } and [griefing or theft](standard-procedures.md#327-griefing-or-theft){ data-preview }.

## Who May Jail a Player?

**Admins** may use reasonable judgment to temporarily jail a player without advance Server Lead approval when a specific, credible concern warrants immediate containment. They must still investigate, document the reason, and follow the limits below.

**Moderators** may jail a player when either of the following applies:

- They have established sufficient evidence to support an investigative jail under the applicable procedure.
- An Admin has authorized the jail for the incident, including when evidence is still being gathered.

An Admin's permission to jail does not by itself prove the player committed an offense. The Moderator must know why the hold was authorized, follow the applicable limits, and record which Admin approved it. If the evidence is disputed or the appropriate action is unclear, involve an Admin.

## What Counts as Evidence?

Evidence should help establish **what happened, when and where it happened, and which account was involved**. Examples include:

| Evidence | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| **CoreProtect block or container history** | The correct world, location, time, action, and player account. Check whether the activity was authorized. |
| **Video or screenshots** | The relevant action is visible, the source and time are reasonably reliable, and the player can be identified. |
| **Direct staff observation** | What the staff member actually saw, including the player, action, location, and time. |
| **Plugin or anti-cheat logs** | Whether repeated alerts are supported by player behavior, other records, or technical context. |
| **Player reports and witness statements** | Specific details that can be checked against logs, observations, or other evidence. |

A player report can start an investigation, but **an accusation alone does not establish a violation**. A single anti-cheat alert, an unexplained item, or an image without enough context does not automatically justify jailing someone. Staff must consider alternative explanations, including authorized claim access or technical false positives.

**Sufficient evidence** means the available, checked information reasonably connects a particular player to the suspected conduct and gives staff a specific reason to continue containment. It does not require staff to make a final punishment decision before finishing the investigation. Record what the evidence shows and what remains uncertain.

## Jailing While Evidence Is Still Being Gathered

An Admin may authorize an initial investigative hold when there is a specific, credible reason to suspect an active violation and letting the player continue could cause further harm or interfere with the investigation. A Moderator may carry out this type of hold when an Admin authorizes it.

- **One-hour limit:** If sufficient evidence has **not** been established, the player must be released no later than one hour after the original jail began. Release earlier if the concern is resolved or containment is no longer necessary.
- Start the clock when the player first enters jail. Changing staff members or releasing and re-jailing the player for the same incident does not restart it.
- The fact that staff need more time to search logs or receive a response does not extend the deadline.
- If there is no credible reason to contain the player in the first place, do not jail them simply to investigate a report.

!!! warning "No evidence by the deadline"
    **Unjail the player within one hour if sufficient evidence has not been found.** Continue the investigation without keeping them confined. Do not reset the limit by starting another jail for the same unresolved allegation.

## Jailing When Evidence Has Been Established

When staff have sufficient, verified evidence supporting continued containment, **the one-hour provisional limit is not an automatic maximum**. Staff must instead follow the applicable [Standard Procedure](standard-procedures.md#32-situation-specifics){ data-preview } and consider whether jail remains necessary while the case is resolved.

- Record which evidence supports the hold and why the player needs to remain jailed.
- Use only the authority granted to your role. Moderators should escalate decisions outside Moderator authority to an Admin.
- Review whether containment is still needed as the investigation progresses. Do not leave a player jailed indefinitely simply because evidence exists.
- When a disciplinary decision is made, follow the appropriate warning or ban procedure. Jail is not a substitute for that decision.
- Unjail the player once the hold is no longer justified, including when they have been banned. Verify that the jail state was cleared.

If the evidence, continued confinement, or appropriate duration is disputed or unclear, consult an Admin or Server Lead rather than inventing a new jail duration.

## Required Staff Record and Release

For each jail, document:

1. The player and the server/world involved.
2. The reason for containment and the available evidence or investigation references.
3. The staff member who jailed the player and, when applicable, the Admin who authorized it.
4. When the jail began, including the time zone, and any required provisional deadline.
5. The outcome, further actions, release time, and confirmation that the player is no longer jailed.

Use the [staff record procedures](logging-documentation.md){ data-preview } to preserve the necessary evidence and decisions. Check the correct account before using `/jail` or `/unjail`, and verify the final jail state rather than repeatedly toggling commands.

## When to Escalate

Large-scale exploits, threats to server data, contested evidence, player safety concerns, or allegations involving staff may require Developer or Server Lead involvement under [Standard Procedures](standard-procedures.md){ data-preview }. Preserve the evidence, explain what is known, and identify who is responsible for the next decision. Escalating an incident does not, by itself, authorize an otherwise unsupported jail.
