# 4. Staff Actions & Records

This section outlines **staff expectations for decisions, player communications, records, and escalation**. Act fairly, use verified information, and document what happened so other staff can follow up.

For step-by-step bot instructions, form fields, examples, and record management, see [Learning → Moderators → Module 6: Moderation Records & Disciplinary Actions](../learning/moderators/mod6.md){ data-preview }.

---

## 4.1 Investigate and Decide

- **Check the facts:** Review relevant history and evidence. Separate confirmed information from reports or assumptions.
- **Follow procedure:** Use the [standard procedures](standard-procedures.md#32-situation-specifics){ data-preview } and [chain of command](staff-roles-responsibilities.md#21-chain-of-command){ data-preview }.
- **Use good judgment:** Choose a proportionate response, not the harshest available punishment. Ask an Admin or Server Lead when unsure about a nonurgent issue.
- **Act when needed:** Address immediate risks to players or the server, then document your actions and reasoning.
- **Respect privacy:** Treat players professionally. Keep private registration information and sensitive evidence out of public channels.

## 4.2 Send a Written Warning

- **Use warnings appropriately:** Explain the rule violation, the behavior that must change, and any applicable next steps or consequences.
- **Verify the history:** Confirm the facts before calling something a first or repeated offense.
- **Keep it professional:** Be clear, factual, respectful, and proportionate.
- **Document it:** Record why the warning was issued and whether the message was sent or delivered.
- **Sign only for yourself:** Do not sign or submit a warning in someone else's name.

## 4.3 Send a Disciplinary Action Notice

- **Follow [Section 3.2](standard-procedures.md#32-situation-specifics){ data-preview }:** Apply any required review or escalation before taking action.
- **Explain the action:** Tell the player the reason, restriction, and duration where applicable. Do not state a duration or future penalty that has not been decided.
- **Keep the internal record:** Include the facts, decision, duration, evidence reference, staff involved, and communication outcome.
- **Remember:** A player-facing notice does **not** replace the internal incident record.

## 4.4 Incident Records

- **Log important work:** Record player incidents, technical issues, operational changes, and formal decisions so another staff member can understand and continue the work.
- **Include the essentials:** What happened, when and where, who was involved, verified evidence, action taken, and outstanding follow-up.
- **Choose the right category:**
    - **Player:** Reports, investigations, warnings, disciplinary actions, appeals, and player follow-up.
    - **Plugin:** Bugs, configuration issues, updates, investigations, and fixes.
    - **General:** Outages, cross-system incidents, server-wide changes, and other operational matters.
    - **Communication:** Official player messages, kept separate from internal discussions.
- **Avoid duplicates:** Maintain one primary record per issue; reference related records where needed. Manual case references do not automatically link records.
- **Protect evidence:** Keep screenshots, exports, logs, and sensitive details in approved staff-restricted storage. Record a brief summary and retrievable reference instead of uploading private material to public channels or general notes.
- **Keep records current:** Add significant findings, decisions, and handoffs. Resolve records when work is complete; reopen them when necessary.
- **Use saved records:** Ordinary Discord conversations do **not** replace an official staff record. Never include passwords or unnecessary personal information.

## 4.5 Student Conduct Escalation

- **Escalate serious concerns:** Refer suspected student-conduct violations to the **Server Lead**, who coordinates with the ERRSA advisor or appropriate Housing and Residence Life staff.
- **Preserve facts:** Keep a timeline, relevant username/UUID, actions taken, and secure evidence references.
- **Stay within your role:** Do not promise institutional outcomes, independently expand an investigation beyond your responsibilities, or share conduct information publicly.
- **Record the handoff:** Document who received the issue and any follow-up.

## 4.6 Discord Bot Downtime

- **Requirements still apply:** Outages do not waive documentation, communication, or privacy expectations.
- **Use the restricted fallback:** Contact a Developer or Server Lead to coordinate manual communication and later record restoration.

### 4.6.1 Sending the Email

- Only a **Developer or Server Lead** should send manual notices using the approved template.
- Confirm the recipient and contents before sending. **CC the Server Lead and `dberrsa@erau.edu`**.
- Keep registration information restricted to authorized staff. Do not sign for someone else.

### 4.6.2 Recording the Action

- Save a temporary **staff-restricted** record with the incident, sender, date, notice type, delivery status, evidence references, and follow-up owner.
- Preserve the full sent message in the approved restricted location, not an ordinary Discord chat.

### 4.6.3 Restoring Records After Downtime

- Restore the original communication and internal incident history through the approved administrative process.
- Verify the restored entries, then close the temporary handoff.
- **Never resend a notice just to create a database record.** Escalate failed or uncertain restorations.

## 4.7 Sending Custom Communications

- **Use an appropriate purpose:** Send official messages that do not fit a warning or disciplinary action template.
- **Be professional:** Make the message accurate, clear, respectful, and free of unnecessary private information or staff-only discussions.
- **Keep a record:** Document significant messages and reference the related incident or operational record when applicable.
