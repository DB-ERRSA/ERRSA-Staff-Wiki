# 2. Staff Roles & Responsibilities


## 2.1 Chain of Command

The ERRSA Minecraft Server follows a linear chain of command to ensure smooth operations and accountability.

- Helper → Isn’t held to any staff responsibilities, but can contribute to a specific project, such as organizing events, or building spawn.

- Moderator → Handles immediate in-game issues such as rule enforcement.

- Admin → Escalation point for issues that require higher permissions or judgment, such as investigations or repairs.

- Developer → Handles technical problems, plugin issues, or larger gameplay-related fixes. Can also assist Admins with day-to-day operations or extreme issues.

- Minecraft Committee Lead → Final say on all server-related matters, staff decisions, and direction of the server.

- Executive Board → Provides funding and oversight, but does not intervene in day-to-day server operations unless also part of the MC committee.

Whenever possible, staff should attempt to resolve issues at the lowest level, but never be afraid to escalate issues, especially if you are unsure about the course of action. In certain circumstances, it may be appropriate to escalate issues past the next level of command. For example, if a moderator discovers a game breaking bug, it should be brought directly to a developer, skipping over the admins which would be next in line.

## 2.2 Staff Conduct Expectations

As mentioned before in [Section 1.2](introduction.md#12-expectations){ data-preview }, staff are expected to act kindly and professionally, especially when dealing with conduct.

Act Professionally: Treat all players fairly and respectfully, regardless of personal feelings.

Maintain Confidentiality: Do not share staff discussions, punishments, or internal decisions with players.

Stay Neutral: Avoid favoritism or using staff powers for personal benefit.

Follow Procedures: Apply the [standard procedures](standard-procedures.md#32-situation-specifics){ data-preview }, use Discord for formal notices ([Section 4.2](logging-documentation.md#42-send-a-written-warning){ data-preview } and [Section 4.3](logging-documentation.md#43-send-a-disciplinary-action-notice){ data-preview }), and escalate through the chain of command ([Section 2.1](#21-chain-of-command){ data-preview }).

Be Active & Reliable: Consistently engage with the server and fulfill responsibilities.

Staff members serve as role models for the community. Misuse of power or repeated failure to meet expectations may result in removal from the team.

## 2.3 Staff Roles

The Executive Board provides oversight. The Committee Lead directs the server team; Developers maintain its technical systems; Admins handle escalations; and Moderators resolve day-to-day player issues.


??? info "Moderator"

    **Front-line staff responsible for enforcing rules and maintaining order.**

    **Duties include:**

    - Assisting players with questions or concerns.    
    - Issuing warnings, mutes, kicks, and bans according to procedure.
    - Monitoring gameplay and reporting larger issues to admins or developers.

    **Access:** No gameplay-enhancing permissions; strictly moderation tools.

    A safe, entry-level staff role suitable for community members trusted to enforce rules.


??? info "Admin"

    **Senior moderators with some enhanced permissions.**

    **Duties include:**

    - Handling escalated player issues and investigations.
    - Restoring damaged property or stolen items using limited creative access.
    - Supporting developers with non-technical server management.

    **Access:** Inherits all Moderator permissions plus limited server management tools.


??? info "Developer"

    **Trusted staff responsible for the technical side of the server.**

    **Duties include:**

    - Adding, configuring, or updating plugins & server settings.
    - Handling technical issues or exploits.
    - Developing & maintaining new server features, external tools, and databases.
    - Assisting in moderation issues that require restricted server or database access.

    **Access:** Full server permissions, including all moderation and creation tools.

    **Additional Access:** Apex Hosting control panel, related tools and databases.


??? info "Minecraft Committee Lead"

    **Head administrator and ultimate authority on server-related matters.**

    **Duties include:**

    - Responsible for the entire staff team, including recruitment, training, and removals.
    - Final say on gameplay, rule enforcement, and server direction.
    - Assists in developer and moderation related duties whenever possible.
    - Works directly with the ERRSA Executive Board for communication, funding, and collaboration.

    **Access:** Full server permissions, including all moderation and creation tools.

    **Additional Access:** All ERRSA MC related tools and databases.


??? info "ERRSA Executive Board"

    **Oversees the ERRSA Minecraft project as part of the Embry-Riddle Resident Student Association.**

    **Duties include:**

    - Provides funding, oversight, and long-term guidance.
    - Led by the ERRSA club advisor.
    - Does not directly manage staff or server operations, acting more as a board of trustees.

    **Access:** No direct server permissions.
