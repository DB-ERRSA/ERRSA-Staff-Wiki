# 1. Introduction


## 1.1 Mission

Welcome to the official [ERRSA]("Embry-Riddle Resident Student Association") Minecraft server!

As part of the Embry-Riddle Resident Student Association, our mission is to connect residents and foster a welcoming campus community. Minecraft is one of the many ways we bring people together and make ERAU feel like home. Our goal is to create a fun, fair, and friendly environment where every player can enjoy their experience.

---

## 1.2 Expectations

As an official staff member of the ERRSA MC server, you are both a leader and a role model in our community. With that comes responsibility; Staff are held to the same server rules as all players. Violations will be treated equally, regardless of rank. Staff should act respectfully, fairly, and be helpful at all times. If you are ever unsure how to address a situation, do not guess, please reach out to another staff member who can help. Apply punishments and procedures fairly. Avoid favoritism or bias. Staff are expected to set a positive example, both in chat and in gameplay.

---

## 1.3 Server Rules

The ERRSA MC server has a few simple rules to ensure we maintain a safe, respectful, and enjoyable environment for all players. Please review the rules below:

[1] The Embry-Riddle honor code is upheld at all times

[2] No cheating, hacking, exploiting, spamming, scamming, or otherwise unlawful behavior

[3] No offensive or inappropriate builds, messages, or communications

[4] Do not destroy, steal, or damage another player's builds, property, or creations

[5] Respect other players, no toxicity or flaming, and use appropriate language

[6] Do not advertise other servers, websites, or products

[7] Always follow the instructions of the server staff. Concerns should be taken to the ERRSA Executive Board or Advisor.

[8] In general, use common sense, and if you have questions or concerns reach out to our MC Staff team!
