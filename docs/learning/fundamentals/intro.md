# Introduction

Welcome to the **Server Fundamentals** learning track. This section is required for all ERRSA MC staff members and provides the foundation you need before moving into role-specific training.

---

## What is ERRSA MC?

- **ERRSA MC** is a vanilla-focused SMP server exclusively for Embry-Riddle students.
- The server is developed and maintained by the ERRSA MC Committee.
- ERRSA provides funding and oversight for the project.

More technical details of the server will be discussed in the [next module](mod1.md){ data-preview }.

---

## What staff are expected to understand?

Staff are both guides and enforcers.

As a staff member, you are both a **guide** and an **enforcer**. You should be familiar with the systems appropriate to your role so that you can answer questions, help players, and enforce server rules fairly and accurately.

---

## How the Learning System Works

The Learning section is divided into five tracks:

1. **Server Fundamentals** — Required for all staff.
2. **Moderators** — Role-specific training for Moderators.
3. **Admins** — Role-specific training for Admins.
4. **Developers** — Role-specific training for Developers.
5. **Server Lead** — Role-specific training for the Server Lead.

The Fundamentals track covers the server's general philosophy, rules, systems, procedures, and staff resources without focusing on role-specific responsibilities.

The remaining four tracks provide a nearly complete guide for their respective roles. These tracks are sequential: for example, an Admin should complete the Fundamentals and Moderator tracks before moving into Admin training.

!!! tip "Learning beyond your role"
    At the top of each page are role tags to show which staff role the content is relevant for. Reading material outside your current role is not required, but it is strongly encouraged. Understanding how other parts of the staff team operate can make you a more effective teammate and prepare you for potential future promotions.

---

### The Wiki as a Source of Truth

Each section of the wiki serves a different purpose:

| Section         | Purpose                                                                        |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Documentation   | Staff expectations, standard procedures, and authoritative server information. |
| Learning        | Training material designed to teach staff how the server and its systems work. |
| Tools           | Reference information for external tools used by staff.                        |
| Plugins         | Reference information for specific plugins.                                    |
| Changelog       | A record of server updates and changes.                                        |
| Troubleshooting | Guides for diagnosing common problems.                                         |

!!! warning "Learning vs. reference documentation"
    While learning modules may explain information that also exists elsewhere in the wiki, it should only be treated as a **training resource**, not the authoritative source of truth. When a Learning page points you toward Documentation, Tools, Plugins, or another source of truth, use that reference for the most current information.

---

## What You Will Learn

By completing Server Fundamentals, you should understand:

- The purpose and philosophy of ERRSA MC.
- The server's basic architecture and major features.
- Staff expectations and the server's enforcement philosophy.
- When and how staff should help players.
- The major player-facing systems on the server.
- How staff communicate and document information.
- Where to find additional information.
- When and how to escalate an issue.
