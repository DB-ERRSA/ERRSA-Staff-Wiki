- Enforcement philosophy
    - ERRSA MC likes to employ a relaxed enforcement. a typical rule of thumb is if it isn't hurting a player (ie grieving stealing, harmful language, etc), or hurting the server (economy exploits, lag machines, world griefing, etc) than it's most likely allowed. our intention is never to limit or remove players access to the server, and rules and punishments are only here to ensure the server stays fun, fair, and friendly to everyone. in most situations, a warning or and simple conversation is enough to resolve situations, administrative action should always be a last resort reserved only for serious situations, or repeated offenses. it is also important to distinguish between intention and unintentional actions, as they should not be treated equally. for example, someone making a complex Redstone farm that begins to lag the setver is not the same as someone intentionally making a lag machines for the purpose of making the playing experience worse for others. While the result is the same in both situations, intention tells a lot about how to handle a situation, ie warning, conversation, or action. In more xomplex situations, ERRSA MC operators under a "what is most likely to have happened?" principal. sometimes all the evidence is not clear, so some level of judgement is required.
- When something becomes a staff issue
    - When staff need to get involved is when a direct rule is broken to the philosophy described above. when something kr someone is actively hurting another player or the server. or there is a clear rule violation. Staff do not only handle situations from direct reports. suspicious player behavior, inappropriate builds or signage found in the world, or other related situations also require stsff intervention without a direct report being made.
