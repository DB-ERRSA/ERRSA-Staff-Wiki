# Moderator Assessment

Use these cases to discuss how a Moderator would handle routine reports. This page is a **readiness exercise**; no new mandatory promotion requirement is established by this page alone. The Server Lead determines whether a formal assessment is required for the position.

## 1. A New Rule Report

A player says someone is swearing in public chat, but offers no screenshot. What should you check, how should you communicate, and where do you record a formal action?

??? tip "Suggested approach"
    Check available chat context, explain the rule neutrally, follow [Standard Procedures](../../documentation/standard-procedures.md){ data-preview }, and use the Moderator [staff records process](../moderators/mod6.md){ data-preview }.

## 2. An Unclear Theft Accusation

A player accuses a neighbor of taking diamonds but has no time, world, or chest details. What should you collect, and when do you ask an Admin?

??? tip "Suggested approach"
    Ask for world, coordinates, approximate time, affected container, and people with access. Do not punish on an allegation alone. Escalate investigation requiring Admin tools.

## 3. A Suspicious Anticheat Alert

A single Themis alert appears after a Bedrock player lags. What would you do before any punishment?

??? tip "Suggested approach"
    Treat it as a lead. Look for corroborating evidence, distinguish connection issues from a real exploit, and escalate if necessary. See [Moderator Cheating & Exploits](../moderators/mod4.md){ data-preview }.

## 4. An Upset Player Dispute

Two players argue publicly and demand opposing punishments. How do you calm the situation and preserve evidence?

??? tip "Suggested approach"
    Remain neutral, separate facts from claims, use the least disruptive appropriate measure, and avoid sharing restricted records publicly. See [Moderator Player Disputes](../moderators/mod5.md){ data-preview }.

## 5. A Staff Record

You issued an authorized warning after reviewing evidence. What should the Player record contain?

??? tip "Suggested approach"
    Include the account, rule, verified evidence, relevant time, action taken, communication, and outstanding follow-up. Refer to [Moderator Module 6](../moderators/mod6.md){ data-preview }.
