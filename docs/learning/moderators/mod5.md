- Recognizing suspicious player behavior
    - As mentioned before, reports are not the only time investigstion or administrative action is needed. if you come across anything during your time on the server that seems suspicious, investigate. The server features an anticheat plugin called Themis. while its not perfect, and doesnt catch every type of cheat, as no plugin ever could, it is certainly helpful. As a moderator you'll notice in chat when Themis detects suspicious activity. it's importsnt tk chdck the score and ping of the player. a high score, typically above 100, indicates there is a more probability its a real, and not a false possative (which is very common). a high ping means the player is lagging, and typically causes more false posatives, even sometimes ones with a generally high score. If a player is getting a high score with low ping, that is reason for suspicion and shoumd be investigated. Outside of themis and cheating, suspicious behavior could sometimes be verbal or text communication from a player saying they did or are going to do something thats against the rules. Use your intuition and if anything seems odd, it never hurts to look into it more.
- X-ray
   - while x-ray is difficult to detect even with anticheat, there are clear signs when looking at a player. irregular mining patterns that zig zag in seemingly random directions is cause for suspicion, especially if that mining leads them directly to resources like diamond and iron. typically players mine in rhythmic patterns such as strip mining to maximize their chances of finding ore. deviation from this behavior could be a cause for suspicion. Another indicator is an abundance of wealth, especially in raw materials like diamonds. A significantly higher economy in comparison to other players could also be a cause of suspicion. looking at a players playtime relative to their wealth or resources is also a fair indicator. someone with 1000 diamonds but a top playtime on the server is normal, but someone with 1000 diamonds and only 30 minutes could be suspicious. not all suspicious cases immediately mean guilt, maybe a new player has a friend who gave them a lot of money or gear, just because they have something does not immediately mean they cheated, it's just a sourxe of suspicion.
- Duplication and other exploits
   - similar to xraying, and abundance of rare, hard to farm items, such as maxed out gear, elatras, or nether stars could potentially indicate the use of a duplication bug. again though, always take caution, suspicion does not equal guilt.
- Gathering evidence before taking action
    - whenever possible, gather evidence either by having an admin use investigation tools, or by conducting your own research. It is not always a good idea to ask players directly when you sense something suspicious, as if they truly are guilty, it may encourage them to hide their evidence better as they know the staff team has become suspicious, making the overall investigation harder.
- When to escalate suspected cheating
    - as a moderator, it is quite difficult to investigate and determine actual cases of cheating as you do not have access to creative and spectstor mode, investigstion tools, and other resources. Also investigations could be potentially harmful, causing yoy to lose yoyr own personal items or progress. in modt cases, your job as a moderator is simply to identify there may be suspicious activity, and escalate that issue to an admin or higher who can properly investigate and deal eith the situstion.
- Avoiding false accusations
   - always avoid accusing someone of cheating directly, use verbiage such as "We have reasonably suspicious to believe you're cheating" as there is always room for error or false accusations. Also avoid calling it out too early, until proper evidence can be collected
