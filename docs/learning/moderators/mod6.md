# Module 6 · Moderation Records & Disciplinary Actions

Accurate documentation is an important part of moderation. Staff actions should leave enough information for other staff members to understand what happened, why a decision was made, and what action was taken.

---

## Staff Action Records

Staff actions should be recorded so there is a reliable history of moderation decisions.

Records can provide context for:

- Previous warnings.
- Previous disciplinary actions.
- Repeated violations.
- Ongoing incidents.
- Decisions made by other staff members.

Use the appropriate staff action tools and procedures when creating or reviewing records.

---

## Official Written Warnings

Formal warnings provide a documented communication to a player when a situation constitutes a direct rule violation.

Formal warnings should clearly communicate:

- What the player did.
- Which rule or expectation applies.
- What the player should do differently.

Formal warnings are sent to the player's student email through the staff action process.

!!! important "Keep the communication clear"
    A warning should help the player understand what happened and how to avoid repeating the issue. Avoid unnecessary hostility or vague explanations.

---

## Disciplinary Action Notices

When an administrative action is issued, the associated communication should clearly explain the reason for the action.

This is particularly important for:

- Mutes.
- Kicks.
- Bans.
- Other disciplinary actions.

The action should follow the applicable standard procedure and use the appropriate duration.

---

## Documenting Evidence & Context

A formal action notice may not contain enough space for all relevant information.

Document additional context when necessary, including:

- Evidence reviewed.
- Statements from involved players.
- Relevant circumstances.
- Previous actions.
- Reasoning behind the decision.
- Information that may be useful to staff handling a future incident.

The goal is to create a record that another staff member can understand without needing to reconstruct the entire incident from memory.

---

## Using the Staff Action Process

Staff actions should be processed through the appropriate staff tools and procedures.

The ERRSA Discord staff tools can be used to submit formal actions and add information to the staff action system.

!!! info "Use the established process"
    Do not create informal or undocumented disciplinary actions when the established staff process applies.

For the exact current process and available tools, refer to the [Staff Actions & Records](../../documentation/logging-documentation.md) section of the Staff Handbook.

---

## Escalating Serious or Repeated Violations

Serious or repeated violations may require additional staff involvement.

Escalate when:

- The situation exceeds your role's authority.
- Investigation tools are required.
- The violation is severe.
- The player has repeated previous violations.
- The appropriate response is unclear.
- The situation requires a Server Lead decision.

!!! tip "When in doubt, document and ask"
    Good documentation makes escalation easier. If you are unsure how to proceed, record the relevant information and ask an Admin or Server Lead for assistance.

---

## Moderator Reference

Before taking or recording administrative action, make sure you can answer:

- **What happened?**
- **Which rule or procedure applies?**
- **What evidence supports the conclusion?**
- **What action is appropriate?**
- **Has the action been communicated clearly?**
- **Has everything relevant been documented?**
- **Does this need to be escalated?**

These questions provide a useful final check before completing a moderation incident.
