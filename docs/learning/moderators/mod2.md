# Module 2 · Handling Player Reports

Player reports are the primary way players communicate problems to the staff team. A good Moderator should know how to review a report, determine what information is missing, gather additional context, and escalate the situation when necessary.

---

## Receiving and Reviewing Reports

Players can find the report form through the in-game `/report` command or get a direct link from the Discord Minecraft welcome channel.

After submission, reports are sent directly to the Discord staff channel for review.

Reports can cover many different topics, including:

- Player issues.
- Rule violations.
- Server bugs.
- Other problems requiring staff attention.

Because reports can vary significantly in complexity, the first step is determining which staff role is best suited to handle the situation.

!!! tip "More input is better"
    It is always okay to offer help to another staff member, even when the situation is above your current role's abilities. Watching how more experienced staff handle difficult situations is also a valuable learning opportunity.

---

## Getting More Information

Sometimes the initial report may not contain enough information to understand the full situation.

When possible, contact the reporting player and ask for additional information relevant to the situation such as:

- What happened
- Where it happened
- When it happened
- Which players were involved
- Screenshots or other evidence
- Relevant context leading up to the incident

Typically, when a report goes beyond a simple fix or immediate issue and requires some level of investigation, an escalation to a higher role is going to be needed.

!!! important "Get both sides"
    In complicated situations, especially player disputes, do not rely solely on the reporting player's version of events. Whenever possible, hear from all involved parties and compare their accounts against the available evidence.

---

## Collecting Relevant Evidence

Evidence should be used to build a complete picture of the situation.

Depending on the incident, this may include:

- Player statements
- Screenshots
- Timestamps
- Server information
- Relevant logs
- Investigation results from administrative tools

Moderators may not have access to all investigation tools. When those tools are required, escalate the situation as needed.

---

## Determining Appropriate Action

Once enough information has been gathered and the situation is understood, determine whether a rule violation occurred and what response is appropriate.

Use the [standard procedure](../../documentation/standard-procedures.md#32-situation-specifics){ data-preview } in the staff documentation to determine:

1. Whether action is necessary
2. If this is a repeated offense
3. What action is appropriate
4. How long the action should last
5. What needs to be documented

!!! tip "Repeated offenses"
    The `/staff search <username>` command can be used in the Discord staff channel to view previous logs and determine if an offense is repeated.

When the situation is unclear, do not guess. Ask another staff member for help or escalate the situation.

---

## Player Communication and Incidents Recording 

Documentation should happen throughout the entire process, not only after a final action is taken.

Record relevant information from:

- The original report
- Additional information gathered from players
- Evidence collected during the investigation
- Communication with involved players
- The final decision and resulting action

The `/staff` command in Discord is the center for all logging and formal player communication.

For detailed procedures on staff records and logging, see [Staff Actions & Records](../../documentation/logging-documentation.md){ data-preview }, which will be explained in [Module 6](mod6.md#official-written-warnings){ data-preview }.
