# Module 4 · Cheating & Exploits

Cheating and exploits can have a significant impact on the server's fairness and economy. Moderators are often the first staff members to notice suspicious behavior, but they generally do not have the tools required to complete a full investigation.

Your role is to **recognize potential issues, gather what information you can safely obtain, and escalate when appropriate**.

!!! warning "Suspicion is not guilt"
    Suspicious behavior should be investigated carefully. The presence of a suspicious pattern does not automatically mean a player is cheating.

---

## Recognizing Suspicious Player Behavior

Reports are not the only reason to investigate unusual behavior. If you notice something suspicious while playing or moderating the server, it may be appropriate to look into it further.

The server uses **Themis** as an anticheat system. Themis is not perfect and cannot detect every form of cheating, but it can provide useful indicators.

When Themis detects suspicious activity, staff may see a detection in chat.

When reviewing a detection, consider:

- The detection score.
- The player's ping.
- The behavior that triggered the detection.

A high score—typically above 100 in the context described by this training—may make a detection more concerning. High ping can also produce false positives, so the score should not be considered in isolation.

A high score combined with low ping is more reason for suspicion and further investigation.

Suspicious behavior can also come from player communication. A player stating that they have performed or intend to perform something against the rules may warrant additional attention.

---

## X-Ray

X-ray can be difficult to detect, even with an anticheat system.

Potential indicators include unusual mining patterns that consistently lead directly to valuable resources. A player's wealth can also provide context.

For example, a player with a very large quantity of diamonds and extensive playtime may not be unusual. A new player with an unusually large quantity of rare resources and very little playtime may warrant additional investigation.

!!! important "Context matters"
    Having large amounts of valuable resources is not proof of cheating. A player may have received items from a friend or obtained them legitimately. Treat unusual wealth as a reason to investigate, not as proof of guilt.

---

## Duplication & Other Exploits

Duplication bugs and other exploits can produce unusually large quantities of rare or difficult-to-obtain items.

Potential indicators include:

- Maxed-out gear.
- Elytras.
- Nether stars.
- Other rare resources.

As with suspected x-ray use, these indicators should be treated as **suspicion rather than proof**.

---

## Gathering Evidence Before Taking Action

Whenever possible, gather evidence before taking administrative action.

An Admin may be able to use investigation tools to confirm what happened. Moderators can also conduct safe research based on the information available to them.

Be cautious about confronting a player too early. If the player is actually cheating, revealing that staff are suspicious may encourage them to hide evidence or change their behavior, making the investigation more difficult.

---

## When to Escalate Suspected Cheating

Moderators generally do not have access to all the tools needed to investigate suspected cheating.

An Admin or higher may have access to:

- Investigation tools.
- Creative mode.
- Spectator mode.
- Other resources needed to investigate the situation safely.

In most cases, a Moderator's role is to identify suspicious activity and escalate it to an Admin or higher for proper investigation.

!!! tip "Protect yourself too"
    Investigations can potentially put your own items or progress at risk. Do not put yourself in a dangerous situation simply to investigate suspected cheating.

---

## Avoiding False Accusations

Never directly accuse a player of cheating based solely on suspicion.

Instead, use neutral language that leaves room for the possibility of an incorrect conclusion.

For example:

> "We have reasonable suspicion to believe you may be using prohibited tools."

Avoid calling out suspected cheating before sufficient evidence has been collected whenever possible.
