# Module 4 · Cheating & Exploits

Cheating and exploits can have a significant impact on the server's fairness and economy. Moderators are often the first staff members to notice suspicious behavior, but they generally do not have the tools required to complete a full investigation.

Your role is to **recognize potential issues, gather what information you can safely obtain, and escalate when appropriate**.

!!! warning "Suspicion is not guilt"
    Suspicious behavior should be investigated carefully. The presence of a suspicious pattern does not automatically mean a player is cheating.

---

## Recognizing Suspicious Player Behavior

Reports are not the only reason to investigate unusual behavior. If you notice something suspicious while playing or moderating the server, it may be appropriate to look into it further.

The server uses a plugin called [Themis](../../plugins/permissions-and-moderation/themis.md "Click here to visit the plugin page") as an anticheat system. Themis is not perfect and cannot detect every form of cheating, but it can provide useful indicators.

When Themis detects suspicious activity, staff may see a detection in chat:

![themis_example.png](../../assets/images/themis_example.png)

When reviewing a detection, consider:

- The detection score
- The player's ping
- The server's [TPS]("Ticks Per Second, measures the speed and health of the server's processes. A normal value should be close to 20.")
- The behavior that triggered the detection

False-positives are common with anti-cheat plugins, so never take a flag at face value.

The score indicates the likelihood of the flag being real. A high score, typically above 100, may be worth looking into. High ping can also produce false-positives due to the player [rubberbanding]("Ping rubberbanding is a networking glitch where your character moves forward, then suddenly snaps or teleports back to a previous position, like being pulled back by a stretched rubber band"), so the score should not be considered in isolation.

A high score combined with low ping is more of a reason for suspicion and further investigation. Though even in these cases the flag could be wrong.

Outside of reports or Themis, suspicious behavior can also come directly from player communication. A player stating that they have performed or intend to perform something against the rules may warrant additional attention.

!!! tip "The main takeaway"
    Use your intuition, if anything seems odd it never hurts to investigate more.

## X-Ray

[X-ray]("X-raying in Minecraft is the act of using custom resource packs, mods, or game glitches to make common blocks (like stone, dirt, and gravel) transparent or invisible so players can see through the ground and find ores, resources, and hidden structures with ease.") resource packs, exploits, and mods can be difficult to detect, even with a good anticheat system.

The two biggest indicators to look for are unusual mining patterns, and an abundance of wealth.

For example, a player with a very large quantity of diamonds or in-game currency, along with extensive playtime, may not be unusual. However, a new player with an unusually large quantity of rare resources and very little playtime may warrant some suspicion.

Irregular mining patterns that zigzag in seemingly random directions, especially if they  lead  directly to resources like diamond and iron, is cause for suspicion. Typically, players strip mine in predictable patterns to maximize their chances of finding ore. Deviation from this behavior could be a potential cause for suspicion.

!!! important "Context matters"
    Having large amounts of valuable resources is not proof of cheating. A new player may have received items from a friend or obtained them legitimately. Treat unusual wealth as a potential reason to investigate, not as proof of guilt.

## Duplication & Other Exploits

Duplication bugs and other exploits can produce unusually large quantities of rare or difficult-to-obtain items such as:

- Maxed-out enchanted gear
- Elytras
- Nether stars
- Other rare resources

Typically, duplication bugs involve some kind of container. So having multiple shulker boxes, minecarts, or other containers full of rare items in the same slots could indicate they were duplicated.

As with suspected x-ray use, these indicators should be treated as suspicion rather than proof.

---

## Gathering Evidence Before Taking Action

Whenever possible, always gather evidence before taking administrative action.

An Admin may be able to use investigation tools to confirm what happened. Moderators can also conduct safe research based on the information available to them.

Be cautious about confronting a player too early. If the player is actually cheating, revealing that staff are suspicious may encourage them to hide evidence or change their behavior, making the investigation more difficult.

---

## When to Escalate Suspected Cheating

Moderators generally do not have access to all the tools needed to investigate suspected cheating properly or safely.

An Admin or higher may have access to:

- Investigation tools
- Creative mode
- Spectator mode
- Other resources needed to investigate the situation safely

In most cases, a Moderator's role is to simply **identify** suspicious activity and escalate it for proper investigation.

!!! tip "Protect yourself too"
    Investigations can potentially put your own items or progress at risk. Do not put yourself in a dangerous situation simply to investigate suspected cheating.

---

## Avoiding False Accusations

Never directly accuse a player of cheating based solely on suspicion.

Instead, use neutral language that leaves room for the possibility of an incorrect conclusion. For example:

> "We have reasonable suspicion to believe you may be using prohibited tools or exploits."

Avoid calling out suspected cheating before sufficient evidence has been collected whenever possible.
