# Module 3 · Interpreting the Rules

Not every situation on the server fits neatly into a single rule. Moderators must use judgment when determining whether behavior constitutes a violation and how serious the situation is.

!!! important "Use judgment"
    There is no way to describe every possible situation. Consider the circumstances, intent, repetition, impact, and available evidence when determining whether action is appropriate.

---

## Inappropriate Language

Inappropriate language is prohibited under the university-related rules that ERRSA MC follows.

The server's chat filter catches many common cases, but repeatedly attempting to bypass the filter is itself a rule violation.

Most individual cases will not require more than a warning, but more serious language—including slurs or derogatory terms—or repeated attempts to bypass the filter should be handled according to the applicable standard procedure.

Inappropriate language can occur through:

- Text chat.
- Voice chat.
- Signs.
- Books.
- Item names.
- Other forms of player communication.

---

## Harassment & Toxicity

**Harassment** involves targeting an individual verbally or physically. This may include:

- Sending unwanted messages.
- Repeatedly attacking a player.
- Preventing someone from building.
- Continuing behavior after a player has established a boundary.

There is an important distinction between friendly banter and harassment. A situation becomes more concerning when someone is clearly uncomfortable and their boundary is ignored.

**Toxicity** does not necessarily target a specific player. It can include persistent negativity, flaming, berating, or rude behavior that negatively affects the community.

!!! tip "Remember the goal"
    ERRSA MC is intended to build a community. Moderation should help maintain an environment where players can enjoy the server together.

---

## Spam & Advertising

**Spam** is excessive or repeated communication or actions.

Common examples include:

- Sending many chat messages in rapid succession.
- Repeatedly filling another player's inventory with unwanted items.
- Other repeated behavior intended to disrupt communication or gameplay.

**Advertising** means promoting or recruiting for non-ERRSA-affiliated servers, clubs, events, or similar communities.

Advertising is not permitted on the Minecraft server. ERRSA Discord does, however, provide a designated **Self Promotion** channel where appropriate self-promotion is allowed.

---

## Inappropriate Chat, Signs & Books

Inappropriate content can appear through more than normal chat.

Moderators should consider the same standards when inappropriate material appears in:

- Chat.
- Signs.
- Books.
- Item names.
- Other visible player-created content.

Use the applicable standard procedure when the situation is a clear or repeated violation.

---

## Handling Repeated Communication Violations

Repeated communication violations should be evaluated based on the behavior and circumstances rather than treated as isolated incidents.

Consider:

- Whether the player has already been warned.
- Whether the behavior is continuing.
- Whether the behavior is intentionally bypassing a restriction.
- How much the behavior is affecting other players.

Use the applicable standard procedure to determine the appropriate action.

---

## Griefing & Theft

**Griefing** is any unwanted disturbance to another player's property or gameplay.

This can include:

- Destroying another player's build.
- Flooding an area.
- Spawning excessive mobs to cause chaos.
- Repeatedly interfering with someone who is trying to build or mine.
- Other intentional behavior designed to disrupt another player.

Not every technically disruptive action is a serious violation. Repeated behavior after being asked to stop can change the situation significantly.

**Theft** involves taking another player's items, equipment, or structures without permission. This may include taking items from a chest, taking resources from another player's farm, or mining materials from their house.

!!! important "Consider the circumstances"
    Intention, repetition, relationship between the players, and the impact of the behavior all matter when determining whether action is appropriate.

---

## Claim Ownership & Boundaries

The server uses claims to protect player property, but the wilderness is not a free-for-all.

Players have an implied reasonable boundary around their property. Intentionally building a giant wall around someone's house or exploding the terrain around their property can still constitute griefing even when it does not occur directly inside a claim.

Similarly, an unclaimed build is **not automatically free to grief or steal from**. The rules still apply.

Griefing can also target the wider world, such as intentionally flooding or exploding an area for the purpose of destruction.

!!! info "Use reasonable judgment"
    Legitimate activities such as building farm perimeters or mining for resources are not automatically griefing. Consider the purpose and circumstances of the activity.

---

## Identifying Responsible Players & Restoring Damage

Admins have access to tools that can help determine:

- What items were taken.
- Which blocks were broken.
- Who performed an action.
- When the action occurred.

If an investigation or restoration is required, escalate the situation to an Admin.

!!! tip "Escalation is expected"
    You do not need to solve every situation yourself. Asking an Admin for investigation support is part of effective moderation.
