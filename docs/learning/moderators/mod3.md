So what actually constitutes as tule violations, and how can you determine what is major vs minor incident? while there isn't a way to describe every possible situation, and some level of judgement is always required, here are some general guidelines to follow.
- Inappropriate language
    - Inappropriate language is a rule university issued rule that we follow to adhere to the honor code. this is partially due to getting our funding from ERRSA, who gets theirs from the SGA and housing. While we have a chat filter on the server to catch 90% of cases, repeatedly attempting to grt around this system is considered a rule violation. while most situstions are not serious, or require much attention beyond a warning, excessive attempts to get around the filter or more serious language like slurs and derogatory terms should be handeled following the  section 3.2.3? of the stsff handbook. Inappropriate langage can be over voice chat, text chat, signs, books, item names, or other ways of communication 
- Harassment and toxicity
    - Harassment is defined as targeting an individual either verbally or physically. this could include sending unwarranted messages, attacking them in game, or preventing them from building. there is a line between banter among friends, and actually Harassment. typically this is when a person is clearly uncomfortable woth the action or has set a boundary that has been ignored. toxicity is not necessary target at a specific person, but involves general negativity, flaming, berating, or just rude behavior that brings spirits down. Again, remember the principle of the server, building community!
- Spam and advertising
   - Spam is defined as excessive or repeated communications or actions. Typically this is done by sending many messages in chat in a quick succession, but may be done in other ways such as constantly filling someones inventory eith junk items. Advertising is simply promoting or recruiting for non ERRSA affiliated servers, clubs, events, etc. while this is jot allowed on the server, there is a specific channel called "self promotion" on Discord where this is allowed.
- Inappropriate chat, signs, and books
    - defined above
- Handling repeated communication violations
    - defined above
- Griefing and theft
    - griefing is defined as any unwanted disturbance to another player. The most infamous one would be destroying another players build, but it can include things like flooding an area, spawning an excessive amount of mobs to cause chaos, or being a nuisance by getting in people's way to prevent them from building or mining. Again it's important to put thought here, intention, repeated offenses, and relation are all important things to consider when deciding whether tk take action. braking someone's crafting table while they're using is technically griefing, but its not a severe situation on its own to warrant action. now doing it 20 times in a row when they told you to stop, potentially is. Theft is similar, and is defined as stealing another players items, equipment, or structures. this could be items frkma chest, their villages from their farm, or even mining their house for resources, whixh would be griefing and theft.
- Claim ownership and boundaries
    - while the server uses claims, this does not mean the wilderness is a free for all. There is an implied grace period around someones claim. for example, building a giant wall, or exploding the terrain around someones house is still considered griefing, even if its not directly on their property. similarly, if a player has their build unclaimed, intentionally or unintentionally, that does not mean it is free for griefing or stealing. the rule applies regardless. Griefing also doesn't have to be directly against a player, it may be griefing parts of the world, such as flooding or exploding an area for seemingly no other purpose then to destroy it. thinfs like farm perimeters and mining for resources are exceptions to this, and reasoning should be used as always.
- Identifying responsible players/Restoring damage (escalation to admin)
    - If you're ever unsure about a situation, there are tools that admins have access to for seeing what items were taken or blocks broken, by who and when it happened. these investigation tools can be incredibly helpful for determining situstions. never be afriad to ask for support when needed.
