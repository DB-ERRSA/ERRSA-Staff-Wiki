# Moderators

Welcome to the **Moderator** learning track. Moderators are the front line of the ERRSA MC staff team, helping players, addressing rule violations, and escalating situations that require additional tools or authority.

This track builds on the knowledge covered in [Server Fundamentals](../fundamentals/intro.md) and provides the practical knowledge needed to moderate the server effectively.

!!! important "Complete the Fundamentals first"
    The Moderator track assumes you understand the server's general rules, philosophy, systems, and staff expectations. Complete **Server Fundamentals** before beginning this track.

---

## What Moderators Do

Moderators are typically the first staff members to encounter player issues. Your primary role is to be a resource and guide for players: answer questions, clarify rules, point players toward the right resources, and help resolve problems.

Moderators may also need to address immediate issues or disputes when player behavior begins to violate server rules or negatively affect others.

---

## Moderator Permissions & Limitations

Moderators have relatively few permissions beyond those available to regular players, but they can take administrative actions such as:

- Warning players.
- Muting players.
- Kicking players.
- Banning players.

These tools should be used carefully and only when appropriate.

---

## Moderation Expectations

Moderators are expected to follow the same staff communication principles and server philosophy covered in the Fundamentals track.

When taking action:

- Remain calm, neutral, polite, friendly, and professional.
- Follow the applicable server procedure.
- Clearly explain the reason for an action.
- Document actions and relevant context.
- Avoid using staff powers for personal reasons or entertainment.
- Ask for help whenever you are unsure.

!!! warning "Staff are not above the rules"
    Staff members are held to the same standards as players, and in some situations to a higher standard because of their position of responsibility.

For additional expectations, review the relevant sections of the [Staff Handbook](../../documentation/staff-roles-responsibilities.md) and [Standard Procedures](../../documentation/standard-procedures.md).

---

## When to Escalate

Moderators can take administrative action, but they do not have the tools required for every situation.

Escalate to an **Admin** or **Server Lead** when a situation requires:

- Investigation tools.
- Restoration of damaged or stolen property.
- Creative or spectator access.
- Advanced technical investigation.
- Administrative decisions beyond your role.

!!! tip "When in doubt, ask"
    If you need help or are unsure how to handle a situation, asking another staff member is always better than guessing.

---

## Moderator Learning Path

The Moderator track progresses from basic moderation actions into increasingly complex situations:

1. **Moderation Fundamentals** — Understand moderation actions, choosing the appropriate response, communicating decisions, de-escalating situations, and using staff powers responsibly.
2. **Handling Player Reports** — Learn how to review reports, gather information and evidence, determine appropriate action, and document incidents.
3. **Interpreting the Rules** — Learn how to identify common violations and determine how serious a situation is.
4. **Cheating & Exploits** — Learn to recognize suspicious behavior and escalate potential cheating.
5. **Player Disputes** — Learn when to intervene in player conflicts, remain neutral, and know when to step back or escalate.
6. **Moderation Records & Disciplinary Actions** — Learn how staff actions, warnings, evidence, and disciplinary decisions are formally documented.
