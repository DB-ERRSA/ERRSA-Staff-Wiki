# Investigative Jailing Procedure — Proposed

!!! warning "Pending Server Lead approval"
    This is a **proposed procedure**, not an approved rule. The active [Investigative Jailing Policy](../docs/documentation/investigative-jailing-policy.md) remains pending. Agree on the authorization, evidence, and review limits before publishing this policy or changing Standard Procedures to rely on it.

Jailing is a temporary measure to prevent further harm while an active investigation takes place. It is **not a punishment** and does not count as a prior offense.

## When Jailing Is Appropriate

An investigative jail may be considered only when **both** conditions apply:

1. Staff have credible supporting evidence connecting a player to a possible ongoing violation.
2. Allowing the player to continue normally presents an immediate risk of additional harm or loss of evidence that cannot reasonably be addressed through less restrictive steps.

A player report alone or an unverified Themis alert does not justify jailing. An identified suspect is not automatically a jailed suspect.

## Authorization

- **Mods:** Gather initial information, request Admin approval, and carry out an investigative jail only after it is authorized. Mods do not lead the investigation.
- **Admins:** Review the evidence, determine whether containment is necessary, authorize the jail, and begin the investigation immediately.
- **Server Leads:** Review disputed actions, exceptions, extended detention, and cases involving staff members.

If the authorizing Admin is involved in the underlying dispute, an uninvolved Admin or Server Lead must make the decision.

## During the Investigation

1. Record who authorized the jail, the supporting evidence, the immediate risk, and when detention began.
2. Tell the player that the jail is temporary and does not mean they were found guilty.
3. Begin investigating **immediately** and let the player explain their side of the situation.
4. Preserve evidence without unnecessarily changing inventories, deleting items, or applying separate punishments.
5. Release the player as soon as containment is no longer necessary. Do not keep them jailed because an Admin is offline or someone has not had time to investigate.

!!! warning "No holding jail"
    If an Admin cannot start the investigation immediately, staff should not place a player in investigative jail simply to wait for them. Escalate urgent ongoing harm through the chain of command.

## Review & Release

**Proposed limits for leadership discussion:** An initial review within 15 minutes, a maximum initial detention of 30 minutes, and Server Lead approval for any extension. These durations are **not approved** and must be finalized before this procedure takes effect.

- **No violation established:** Release the player and record the outcome.
- **Evidence inconclusive:** Release once continuing detention is not justified; retain the investigation record.
- **Violation confirmed:** Apply the applicable [Standard Procedures](../docs/documentation/standard-procedures.md) punishment and release the player from jail, including if a ban was issued.
- **Serious exploit:** Escalate to a Server Lead and Developer when appropriate.

Close the incident record with the authorizing Admin, start and release times, evidence reviewed, final decision, and any player communication. Investigative jailing does **not** advance the first/second/third-offense ladder.
